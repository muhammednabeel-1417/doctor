How It Works

From booking to consultation in 4 simple steps

1
📝
Register & Choose Doctor
Create your account and browse available specialists by category, rating, and availability.
2
🎫
Book a Token
Pick a date and time slot. Receive your unique queue token instantly via the platform.
3
📊
Track Your Queue
Watch the live queue update every 3 seconds. Get notified when you're next — no guessing.
4
🎥
Video Consultation
Join a secure Jitsi Meet video call with your doctor. Get prescriptions digitally after.
Features

Everything You Need

A complete digital health platform built for patients, doctors, and administrators

Live Token Queue
Real-time queue tracking that updates every 3 seconds. Know exactly when to join your video call.
Jitsi Video Calls
Secure, browser-based HD video consultations. No app download needed — works in any browser.
Digital Prescriptions
Doctors issue prescriptions digitally. Patients can download and share with any pharmacy.
Smart Scheduling
Doctors set their availability; patients book slots. Automatic conflict prevention built in.
Role-Based Access
Separate dashboards for patients, doctors, and admins — each with the right permissions.
Admin Analytics
Real-time reports on appointments, revenue, doctor performance, and patient satisfaction.
